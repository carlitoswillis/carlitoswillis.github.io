Algo tracker is a practice log for algorithm interviews. Each rep starts
blind — the technique used stays hidden until you log it, so the record
reflects what you actually did rather than what you meant to practice.
Sessions are budgeted in minutes rather than a problem count, and techniques
are ranked by how long they have gone without a rep, so the ones going stale
surface first. The log itself reads like a ruled ledger — a plain, dated
record of reps rather than a dashboard.
